For radiologists & sonologists

Ultrasound Fellowship Program

Premier hands-on fellowship training using state-of-the-art equipment including the GE Voluson Expert 22 — mentored by nationally recognised radiologists.

Applications open for the 2025–2026 batch

For Radiologists (MD/DNB/DMRD)

  • Advanced foetal ultrasound — anomaly scans (TIFFA), growth monitoring, advanced Doppler, NT/NB scans and early pregnancy assessment.
  • Foetal echocardiography — in-depth fetal heart evaluation with 2D/3D/4D echo and diagnosis of congenital heart defects. Available only to fellows who have completed at least 6 months of fellowship in foetal and general ultrasound.
  • Specialised modules — neurosonography, musculoskeletal ultrasound and advanced 3D/4D imaging.
  • Clinical practice — high-volume, diverse cases with extensive discussion and independent reporting under supervision.

For Sonologists (MBBS)

  • Comprehensive general ultrasound — abdomen, pelvis, small parts (thyroid, breast) and advanced Doppler.
  • Foetal ultrasound fundamentals — biometry, anomaly screening and monitoring fetal well-being.
  • Practical skills — hands-on scanning technique, patient communication, ethics and protocol optimisation.
  • Mentorship — direct guidance and continuous feedback from senior radiologists.

Eligibility

Radiologists: MD/DNB/DMRD or equivalent. Sonologists: MBBS with recognised ultrasound certification. PNDT: registration with Mann Scanning Centre is mandatory, initiated after the official acceptance letter.

Foetal echo track

Offered only to fellows who have completed at least 6 months of fellowship in foetal and general ultrasound — a higher-level pathway, not entry-level.

Duration & certification

Flexible 6 to 12 months, tailored to the track. A certificate of completion is awarded on successful completion.

Batch size

Limited to 1–2 fellows per batch to ensure personalised attention and hands-on learning.

Program timeline

Application phase

Ongoing (rolling admissions). Submit complete application, document verification and initial screening.

Interview & assessment

1–2 weeks after application. Virtual or in-person interview, technical assessment and reference verification.

Selection & offer

1 week after interview. Final selection, offer letter and program-details confirmation.

Pre-program preparation

2–4 weeks before start. Orientation, equipment familiarisation and study materials.

Program start

6–12 months. Intensive hands-on training, didactic sessions, case discussions and assessments.

Program completion

Final month. Final examination, project submission, certification and career guidance.

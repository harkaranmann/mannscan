About LMP dating

The Last Menstrual Period method is the most common way to calculate pregnancy dates, using the standard 280-day (40-week) pregnancy length from the first day of your last period.

Important notes

  • Estimates assume a regular 28-day cycle.
  • Individual pregnancy lengths may vary.
  • Always consult your healthcare provider for accurate dating.
  • Ultrasound measurements may provide more precise dating.

Recommended scans

  • 6–8 weeks — early pregnancy viability scan
  • 11–14 weeks — NT scan (nuchal translucency)
  • 18–22 weeks — detailed anomaly scan
  • 28–32 weeks — growth scan (if indicated)

GE Signa Explorer 1.5T · with breast coil

Magnetic Resonance Imaging

MRI uses powerful magnetic fields and radio waves to create highly detailed images of organs, soft tissues and bones — without ionising radiation, making it exceptionally safe for repeated imaging.

Clinical applications
  • Brain & spinal cord
  • Joints (knee, shoulder, hip)
  • Abdominal organs
  • Breast imaging
  • Cardiac MRI
  • MR angiography
Preparation
  • Usually no dietary restrictions
  • Fasting may be needed for abdomen
  • Remove all metal objects
  • Inform staff of implants / pacemakers
  • Arrive 30 minutes early
GE Brivo 365 · 64–128 slice

Computed Tomography

Our multi-slice CT scanner provides rapid, detailed cross-sectional imaging with exceptional clarity — ideal for emergencies, trauma and comprehensive diagnostic assessment, with advanced dose-reduction technology.

Diagnostic capabilities
  • Emergency & trauma
  • Chest & lung evaluation
  • Abdominal organ imaging
  • Brain & neurological studies
  • CT angiography
  • Cancer staging
Preparation
  • 4–6 hr fasting for contrast studies
  • Report allergies & kidney issues
  • Remove metal jewellery
  • Kidney function test if contrast needed
  • Comfortable, loose clothing

Full-field digital · with tomosynthesis

Digital Mammography & X-Ray

Full-field digital mammography with tomosynthesis for early breast cancer detection. Low-dose radiation with auto-optimised compression for enhanced comfort and superior image quality, alongside digital radiography.

Applications
  • Annual breast screening
  • Diagnostic mammography
  • Breast biopsy guidance
  • High-risk monitoring
  • Digital chest X-rays
  • Bone & joint X-rays
Preparation
  • No deodorant or powder beforehand
  • Remove all metal jewellery
  • Two-piece clothing recommended
  • Schedule after menstrual period
  • Bring previous reports
Dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry

DEXA — Bone & Body Composition

Precise osteoporosis screening and bone-health assessment following WHO-standard guidelines, with low-dose radiation for accurate mineral-density measurement, fracture-risk and body-composition analysis.

Clinical uses
  • Osteoporosis screening
  • Fracture-risk assessment
  • Body composition analysis
  • Postmenopausal monitoring
  • Steroid therapy monitoring
  • Metabolic bone disease
Preparation
  • No calcium supplements 24 hrs before
  • Light, comfortable clothing
  • Remove metal objects
  • 5–30 minute scan
  • No dietary restrictions

About our centre

Where expertise meets excellence in imaging

For over two decades, Mann Scanning Centre has delivered precise diagnostic imaging with the highest standard of patient care in Jalandhar, Punjab.

Our philosophy

Personalised, precise, compassionate

At Mann Scanning Centre, we are committed to delivering accurate diagnostic imaging with the highest standard of patient care. Our experienced team ensures that every scan is personalised to meet each patient's unique needs — combining advanced technology with genuine human warmth.

We believe a diagnosis is more than an image. It is the first step in a patient's journey, and we treat it with the care and rigour it deserves.
